# Active Plan — Representative cognitive-decompression experiment

- Active hypothesis: decomposing dense source prose into small grounded knowledge nodes plus explicit empty gaps will break rewrite imitation and provide a better substrate for later teaching synthesis than direct rewriting.
- Current approach: source units and graph nodes are immutable knowledge authorities. Teaching synthesis creates traceable teaching records, expands dense source records, iteratively adds missing prerequisite nodes through the configured reader baseline, prunes graph-variant duplicates/navigation, and assembles records deterministically in dependency order.
- Validation path: require 100% retained source-record mapping; exact citation-label retention; every dense source record expanded; zero pending expansion records; zero dense prose; visible issue comments for unresolved prerequisites/gaps; no source contraction; and output that is pedagogically distinct from the source.
- Current checkpoint: outward decompression is complete and accepted for Delta and Qbits. Delta expands 8,558 source words to 10,719 visible teaching words (1.25×); Qbits expands 202,871 to 283,568 (1.40×). Both map every retained source record, expand every dense record, retain all citation labels, have zero dense paragraphs and zero pending expansion records.
- Negative-memory constraints: do not call preservation alone decompression; do not count graph-variant duplicates/navigation as expansion; do not accept structure improvements that remove source knowledge; do not let a free-form author choose which source records enter the document.
- Assignment: primary agent owns integration and validation; no delegation requested.
